Behind the events

I've spent more than a decade working behind the scenes to make productions and experiences happen.

My background started in television, where I worked on shows including The Chew and The Dr. Oz Show, before moving into digital production with Eater.

At Eater, I coordinated more than 50 productions across the country, working with chefs, restaurant owners, crews, vendors and creative teams while managing travel, locations, equipment and day-of production.

Today, I'm bringing that same production mindset into live events, brand activations and talent coordination. I like being the person who keeps everything moving, solves problems when they happen and makes sure everyone knows what needs to happen next.
