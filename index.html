The Yard

Our home base in the Warehouse District: a 12,000 square foot former produce depot two blocks from the light rail. Studio, fabrication shop and a 600-person event floor under one roof. It hosts our Sunset Sessions after-parties and most of our corporate work.411 E Buchanan St, Phoenix, AZ 85004

Get directions

The Ranch

Forty acres of open Sonoran desert at the foot of the McDowell Mountains: a permanent stage, a shaded pavilion and enough sky for every drone show we have ever dreamed up. Open-air season runs October through April, with Stargazer-style dinners under the stars in between.28600 N Pima Rd, Scottsdale, AZ 85266
